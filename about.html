Hi, I'm Anne Sophie.

I'm a UX designer with a Master of Science in Digital Design and Interactive Technologies from the IT University of Copenhagen, where I researched and designed for real operational environments.

My approach grounds every design decision in user research, then uses AI-augmented analysis and prototyping to move quickly from insight to a tested concept.

Having worked frontline in hospitality, I understand both the guest experience and the operational reality behind it.
